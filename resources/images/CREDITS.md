## Note on this asset and the "no AI" positioning

The site's headline claim is **"0% AI-gegenereerde vragen"** — scoped to exam content
(questions, rubrics, model answers), which is authored and reviewed by the NT2 docent. This
decorative hero image is AI-generated and does **not** contradict that claim.

Keep the distinction clean if the copy ever changes: a broader claim such as "no AI anywhere
on this site" would be false while this asset is in use. Swapping in a real photograph would
remove the ambiguity entirely, and is worth doing if the brand leans harder on the anti-AI
message.
