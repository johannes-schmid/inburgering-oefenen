## The structural finding: two kinds of SERP

**Tool SERPs** — `inburgeringsexamen oefenen`, `oefenexamen inburgering A2`,
`inburgering examen oefenen lezen`. Google returns exam pages and download indexes: DUO
(150–400 words) and `nt2taalmenu.nl` (~150 words per exam) sitting at the top on authority and
URL volume. **A blog post cannot win these.** They belong to `/oefenen/[skill]` and
`/oefenexamen/[skill]`.

**Explanatory SERPs** — `hoeveel vragen`, `verschil A1 A2`, `zakken`, `tips`, `kosten`. Google
returns thin language-school pages plus one short DUO page. **This is where the blog wins**, and
every keyword targeted below is one of these.

## Clusters and word-count targets

Targets derive from the current top 3. **They are low. Do not pad** — DUO ranks at 300 words.

### Cluster 1 — PILLAR: the A2 exam explained
`inburgeringsexamen a2` + #3, #2, #20, #19, #22 · **1,600–2,000 words** · explainer with a
four-component comparison table (the AI-Overview bait), per-skill H2s, jump links, FAQ. Links to
every spoke.

### Cluster 2 — SPOKES, one per skill
Same template ×4. **Lezen and Luisteren now** (both live); Schrijven and Spreken when those ship.
- **Lezen** — #10 + #18, #2, #24 · 1,300–1,600
- **Luisteren** — #11 + #17, #24 · 1,300–1,600 · embed audio; YouTube owns this SERP
- **Schrijven** — #12 + #14, #16 · 1,400–1,800 · model answers are the differentiator
- **Spreken** — #13 + #15 · 1,400–1,800 · video effectively required

### Cluster 3 — taalniveaus
`wat is het verschil tussen a1 en a2` + #5, #25, #26, #22 · **1,400–1,700** · CEFR table
(skill × level) + a "welk niveau heb jij nodig" decision block.

### Cluster 4 — zakken, herkansen, uitslag
`inburgeringsexamen zakken` + #7, #8, #9 · **1,200–1,500** · problem→answer, retake table,
timeline. **Highest conversion intent in the plan.**

### Cluster 5 — Wet inburgering 2021 (DEFERRED)
`wet inburgering 2021` + leerroutes, termijn, PVT, MAP · **1,800–2,200** · realistic ceiling
position 4–8; rijksoverheid/divosa/schulinck lock it. Also **blocked**: the statute was
consolidated 2025-10-21 and the change list must be read first (`facts.md` §9).
