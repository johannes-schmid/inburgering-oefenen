inburgeringoefenen.nl · graphic language v2

Dutch Horizon element library

Every element below is built from four primitives — the gable house, the disc, the band and the dot field — recoloured from the Civic palette. Houses always run left to right along the bottom edge as a continuous canal-house skyline. No borders, no drop shadows, no drawn illustration. Depth comes from tonal layering alone.

Sections 14–18 are the icon layer: construction rules, the five real Amsterdam gable types, the canal-house row at every size, and a set of unmistakably Dutch objects. Sections 01–08 are the base kit. Sections 09–13 cover the all-in-one scope: the A2 language exam (Lezen, Luisteren, Schrijven, with Spreken to come), KNM, ONA on the roadmap, and the NT2-teacher trust layer that separates us from AI-generated competitors.

14

Icon construction

72px tile · 10px keepline · 4px unit grid · one orange accent maximum
Tile & keepline
Geometry lives inside the 52px core. Only the skyline base may touch the bottom edge.
Weights
At 72px: structure 8, detail 6, hairline 4. Below that, scale the whole ramp proportionally — 3px at 48, 2px at 24 — never mix scales in one icon.
Corners
3px on masonry, full round on roofs, discs and wheels. Never mix within one form.
Negative space
Windows and doors are cut in the tile colour, never drawn in a lighter blue.

Spout gable. The workhorse — use it for filler houses in long rows.

Icon tiles & states

tile radius is always a quarter of the tile — reward orange only on completion
Rest
Active
Reward
Locked
Round tile
18px at 72, 12 at 48, 8 at 32, 6 at 24. Round tiles only for avatars, level medals and map pins. On navy and on orange the houses invert: the silhouette takes the on-colour and the cut detail takes the tile colour, so the row still reads at a glance.
Rules for the kit
  • One accent disc per composition — the orange is a pointer, not a texture.
  • The skyline always spans the full width, left to right, anchored to the bottom edge and clipped by overflow: hidden — never a lone house floating in space.
  • Three gable types only: dome (999px 999px 0 0, 66% width), bell (full width, 5–9px radius) and stepped (two stacked bands). Alternate them; never repeat the same gable three times in a row.
  • Heights and tints vary per house (5–18% white on primary); the tallest house sits off-centre.
  • Boundaries by tonal shift only. Selected/focus states use inset box-shadow, never a border.
